## 0. TL;DR (the honest bottom line)

- **The paper's contribution is the *methodology + deployment engineering*, NOT an
  accuracy win.** The data does not support "FLEO beats the baseline":
  - FER2013 (YOLOv12-S): FLEO full graph beats baseline by a *marginal* +0.5 acc /

  attention-free backbone (**YOLOv8n**) for a single DPU subgraph.
- The FPGA board measurements (resources, timing, power, FPS) are still **pending a
  physical ZCU104**.

## 1. Project recap (what FLEO is)

A YOLO facial-emotion detector whose P3/P4 neck embeds **FLEO**: a block that splits
features into 7 per-emotion subspaces and enforces mutual orthogonality via
**Gram–Schmidt (GS)** during training. GS is useful at training but hostile to the
DPU (division, reciprocal-sqrt, sequential recurrence). Three deployment routes
resolve this:

| Route | What happens to GS at export | DPU |
|---|---|---|
| **R1 — fold-out** | deleted (identity); rely on trained weights | single subgraph |
| **R2 — Householder** | replaced by a fixed 1×1 conv (constant-folded) | single subgraph |
| **R3 — hybrid** | kept exact, runs on ARM CPU between two DPU parts | DPU–CPU–DPU |

Decisive quantity: **Δ_fold = Acc(full) − Acc(folded)** — the accuracy price of a
single DPU subgraph.

## 2. Results gathered this session (all real, measured)

### 2.1 FER2013 — YOLOv12-S, imgsz 160, 100 epochs (FP32, n=7178)

| Variant | Accuracy | macro-F1 | mAP50 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline (no FLEO) | **71.38%** | 69.66% | 0.7608 |
| FLEO full (R3) | **71.91%** | 70.46% | 0.7609 |
| FLEO Householder (R2) | 68.86% | 67.08% | — |
| FLEO folded (R1) | 66.80% | 64.27% | — |
| **Δ_fold (full − folded)** | **5.11 pts** | **6.19 pts** | — |

- FLEO full beats baseline by a slim +0.5 acc / +0.8 macro-F1 (concentrated in the
  rare *disgust* class).
- Folded R1 (66.8%) is **below** baseline (71.4%) → the pure fold-out is *not* free
  on this task; R2 is the better deployable route.
- Per-class AP (baseline): happy 0.957, surprise 0.880, disgust 0.749, neutral 0.739,
  angry 0.722, sad 0.656, fear 0.621. mAP50 = 0.761.
- Overfitting: FLEO val/cls_loss rises to ~2.2 (vs baseline 0.74) in the last third.

### 2.2 RAF-DB — YOLOv8n, imgsz 128, 40 epochs

| Variant | mAP50 (best) | mAP50-95 | Precision | Recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Baseline** | **0.810** | **0.765** | 0.809 | 0.715 |
| FLEO | 0.758 | 0.728 | 0.766 | 0.617 |
| **Δ (FLEO − baseline)** | **−5.8 pts** | −3.7 pts | — | — |

- **Baseline beats FLEO on RAF-DB** — FLEO does not help here.
- RAF-DB is cleaner than FER2013 (higher mAP50-95).
- Both overfit after ~epoch 20-25; `best.pt` (best epoch) is what matters.
- FER2013 on YOLOv8n was still training at session end — pending.

### 2.3 Model-intrinsic workload / roofline (host-computed, no board)

| Route | Conv layers | W (GOP) | Q (MB) | I (op/byte) | P_roof (TOPS) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 fold-out | 128 | 3.20 | 12.06 | 265.7 | 1.229 |
| R2 Householder | 130 | 3.21 | 12.09 | 265.2 | 1.229 |
| R3 hybrid | 128 | 3.20 | 12.06 | 265.7 | 1.229 |

- Ridge I* = 64 op/byte → all routes are **compute-bound** (I ≫ I*); DDR bandwidth
  is not the bottleneck. Efficiency will be an array-utilization question.

## 3. The deployment attempt (Vitis AI on Windows)

Setup that worked:
- Docker Desktop + WSL2 on Windows (RTX 2060 present, but Vitis container is CPU).
- Pulled `xilinx/vitis-ai-pytorch-cpu` (Vitis AI 3.5, Python 3.8, torch 1.13.1).
- The checkpoint `best_fleo.pt` is a **main-branch** artifact
  (`fleo.yolo_integration.FLEODetectionModel`) — the feature-branch tools
  (`run_vitis.py`, `FLEOClassifier`) can't load it. **Deployment must use `main`.**

Fixes committed so the flow runs:
- `train_fer2013.py`: added `from __future__ import annotations` (PEP 604 on Py 3.8).
- `deploy/yolo_to_folders.py`: rebuilds raw `train/<emotion>/` folders from the
  YOLO-format dataset (needed by the loaders).
- `deploy/quantize_vai.py`: added `--cfg` (attention-free backbone) and forced
  batch=1 for xmodel export.
- Dataset `data.yaml` `path:` fields still pointed at `/kaggle/...` → must be fixed
  to the local/container path.

What succeeded:
- `scripts.export --route r1` → **"single DPU subgraph expected (R1)"**, onnxruntime↔torch
  parity 2.4e-4 ✅ (the Gram-Schmidt-signature proof at the ONNX level).
- `quantize_vai.py --mode calib` → INT8 calibration ran to completion.

What FAILED (the key finding):
- `quantize_vai.py --mode test` (xmodel export) → **FATAL**:
  `xir::Op ... AAttn ... matmul shape unmatch {32,4,32,25} vs {32,4,32,32}` and
  `aten::silu_ not defined in XIR`.
- **Conclusion: YOLOv12 area-attention (A2C2f/AAttn) does not compile to the
  DPUCZDX8G.** The ONNX-level "single subgraph" heuristic was optimistic — it only
  checked GS-signature ops, not attention.

**Resolution:** switch to **YOLOv8n** (convolution-only, DPU-proven). Retraining on
YOLOv8n started locally (RTX 2060, 6 GB): yolov8n, imgsz 128, batch 16, 40 epochs.
